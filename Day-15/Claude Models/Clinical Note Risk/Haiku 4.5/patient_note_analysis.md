# Patient Note Summary & Risk Analysis

## Summary

**Patient Presentation:** 2-day history of chest pain with stable vital signs. EKG performed and normal. Discharged home on ibuprofen with advice to return if symptoms worsen.

---

## 🚨 Clinical Risks

### HIGH PRIORITY

1. **Missing Troponin Testing**
   - No troponin ordered despite 2 days of ongoing chest pain
   - Normal EKG + stable vitals do NOT exclude acute coronary syndrome (ACS)
   - Troponin is standard of care in chest pain workups
   - **Risk:** Missed MI with delayed diagnosis

2. **Incomplete Evaluation**
   - No documented assessment of cardiac risk factors (age, smoking, family history, etc.)
   - Differential diagnosis not documented (PE, aortic dissection, GI, musculoskeletal causes)
   - No clear safety-netting criteria documented

### MODERATE PRIORITY

3. **Vague Discharge Instructions**
   - "Follow up if symptoms worsen" is non-specific
   - No documented red-flag symptoms explained to patient
   - Unclear what constitutes "worsening" vs. expected natural history

---

## 📋 Documentation Risks

1. **Attribution to External Pressure**
   - Explicit mention of "time pressure due to ED volume" problematic because it may suggest workup was curtailed due to efficiency rather than clinical judgment
   - Creates liability exposure if adverse outcome occurs

2. **Inadequate Documentation**
   - Very brief note lacks detail on clinical decision-making
   - No documented reasoning for discharge without troponin
   - No risk stratification documented

3. **Medicolegal Vulnerability**
   - Discharging chest pain without troponin testing is difficult to defend in litigation, even if patient outcome is benign
   - Explicit documentation of "No troponin ordered" may be interpreted as omission rather than conscious decision

---

## Recommended Actions

- Consider recalling patient for troponin testing if feasible
- Document clear risk stratification in discharge summary
- Ensure follow-up is with specific timeline (24-48 hours) rather than conditional
- Review ED protocols for chest pain workup to ensure troponin testing is routine standard
